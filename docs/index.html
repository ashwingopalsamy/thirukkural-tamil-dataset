Thirukkural Tamil Dataset · திருக்குறள்

The Tamil text of all 1,330 kurals of the Thirukkural (திருக்குறள்), in 133 chapters and three books, with ids tk:0001 to tk:1330. Each kural was compared letter by letter across six digital sources and checked against three 19th-century printed editions where they differ. Each kural lists its sources and a sha256 of its text. Joined to every kural: English translations by G. U. Pope (1886), W. H. Drew and John Lazarus, and V. V. S. Aiyar (1916); Parimelazhagar's Tamil commentary; and a metre analysis of every foot. The core text is licensed CC0-1.0.

Also known as: Tirukkural, Thirukural, Thiruvalluvar's Kural.

Quick start

import pandas as pd

base = "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ashwingopalsamy/thirukkural-tamil-dataset/main"
kurals = pd.read_json(f"{base}/data/core/kurals.jsonl", lines=True)
